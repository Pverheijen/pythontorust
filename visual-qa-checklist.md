# Visual QA Checklist

## Viewports
- Mobile: 375x812
- Tablet: 768x1024
- Desktop: 1280x800+

## Pages
- Home
- Section listing
- Article page

## Verify on light + dark mode
- Heading scale and spacing are consistent.
- Sticky header does not dominate viewport height.
- Breadcrumb truncates gracefully.
- Icon controls are visible and focusable.
- CTA button style is clear and consistent.
- Link lists are easy to scan and click.
- Article line length remains readable.
- Subscription card/input/button hierarchy is clear.
- Related articles list styling is consistent.
- Keyboard focus ring is visible on links/buttons/inputs.
- Reduced motion preference is respected.
